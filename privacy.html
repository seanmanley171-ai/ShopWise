SHOPWISE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

SHOPWISE is published by Manley Inc. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens with your information when you use the SHOPWISE app and website.

The short version

In this version, SHOPWISE has no accounts and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your own device. We do not collect it, sell it, or use it for advertising.

What stays on your device

Your shopping list, price watches, logged purchases, ZIP code, cost-per-mile setting, and preferred stores are stored only in your browser's local storage (localStorage) on your device. That data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to any other server. You can erase all of it at any time in Settings → "Reset all ShopWise data," or by clearing your browser's site data.

No accounts

This version of SHOPWISE does not offer accounts, sign-in, subscriptions, or payments. We do not ask for your name, email address, password, or payment details.

No tracking or analytics

SHOPWISE does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party cookies. The app works offline after its first load and makes no requests to retailer or advertising servers.

Sample price data — please read

All prices, stock counts, distances, and shipping figures shown in this version are sample (demonstration) data, clearly labeled "Sample" in the app. They are not live retailer prices and are not collected from you or from retailers. A future version may connect to real retailer data sources; if that changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated first.

Children

SHOPWISE is a general shopping tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
